# How to close an account

To close an account, your balance must be $0 or positive with no pending transactions. You can
request closure in the app under Account Settings > Close Account, or by contacting support.
Accounts with a negative balance must be brought to $0 before closure can be processed.

If you have automatic payments or direct deposits set up, move them to a new account first —
closing an account does not cancel external payment authorizations, and payments sent to a
closed account will be returned to the sender, which can cause missed bills.

Any remaining balance is issued as a check to the address on file, or transferred to another
account you own, within 5-7 business days of closure. Closing an account does not affect your
credit score, since standard checking and savings accounts are not reported to credit bureaus.
