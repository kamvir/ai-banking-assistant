# KYC requirements — what documents are needed

"Know Your Customer" (KYC) is a legal requirement for all banks to verify the identity of
customers before opening an account, as part of anti-money-laundering (AML) regulations.

To complete KYC, we require:

- A valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport).
- Your full legal name, date of birth, and Social Security number (or ITIN for non-citizens).
- A US residential address (P.O. boxes are not accepted as a primary address).

For business accounts, we additionally require your Employer Identification Number (EIN),
formation documents, and identification for all beneficial owners holding 25% or more of the
business.

KYC is a one-time process per customer; you don't need to repeat it when opening additional
accounts, unless your information has changed or a periodic re-verification is triggered (see
"Updating your KYC information").
