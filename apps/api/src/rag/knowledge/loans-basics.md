# Loan basics

We offer personal loans, auto loans, and home equity lines of credit (HELOC). All loans go
through the same core process: you submit an application with your financial information, we
run a credit check and evaluate eligibility, and if approved, funds are disbursed to your
account (for personal and auto loans) or made available as a credit line (for HELOC).

Key terms to know:

- **Principal** — the amount you borrow.
- **APR (Annual Percentage Rate)** — your total yearly cost of borrowing, including interest and
  most fees, expressed as a percentage.
- **Term** — how long you have to repay the loan, typically 12-84 months for personal loans.
- **Secured vs. unsecured** — a secured loan (like an auto loan) is backed by collateral; an
  unsecured loan (like most personal loans) is not, and typically carries a higher interest rate
  as a result.

Missing payments can negatively affect your credit score and may result in late fees. Contact us
as early as possible if you expect to miss a payment — hardship options may be available.
