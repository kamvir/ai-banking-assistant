# Wire transfer fees

**Domestic outgoing wires:** $25 flat fee, processed same business day if submitted before
2:00 PM local time. Wires submitted after the cutoff are sent the next business day.

**Domestic incoming wires:** free.

**International outgoing wires:** $45 flat fee, plus the exchange rate margin applied at the
time of conversion. Delivery typically takes 1-3 business days depending on the receiving
country and bank.

**International incoming wires:** $15 fee.

Wire transfers cannot be cancelled or reversed once processed, so double-check the recipient's
account and routing details before submitting. Premium checking customers receive one free
domestic outgoing wire per statement cycle.
