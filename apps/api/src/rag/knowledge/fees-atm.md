# ATM fees

Withdrawals at ATMs in our network are always free, with no limit on the number of transactions.

For out-of-network ATMs, we charge a $2.50 fee per withdrawal. The ATM operator may also charge
their own separate surcharge, which we don't control and which is disclosed on the ATM screen
before you confirm the transaction.

Customers with a Premium checking account receive up to 4 out-of-network ATM fee rebates per
statement cycle (our $2.50 fee only — third-party surcharges are not rebated). Standard checking
and savings accounts do not include rebates.

International ATM withdrawals incur an additional 3% foreign transaction fee on top of any
out-of-network fee, based on the transaction amount after currency conversion.
