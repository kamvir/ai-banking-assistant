# How identity verification works

When you apply for an account, we verify your identity using a combination of automated checks
against your ID document, a selfie/liveness check in the app, and cross-referencing your
information with credit bureaus and identity verification databases. This process is handled by
a licensed third-party verification provider and typically completes within a few minutes.

If automated verification can't confirm your identity with confidence, your application is
routed to manual review, which can take 1-2 business days. We may request additional documents,
such as a utility bill for proof of address or a secondary ID.

We do not accept expired IDs. If your ID is within 90 days of expiring, we recommend renewing it
first to avoid delays. Verification failures do not affect your credit score, since identity
checks use a "soft" inquiry, not a hard credit pull.
