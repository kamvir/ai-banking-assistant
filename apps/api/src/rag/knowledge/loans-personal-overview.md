# Personal loan overview

Our personal loans range from $2,000 to $50,000, with terms from 12 to 60 months. They're
unsecured, meaning no collateral is required, and can be used for nearly any purpose — debt
consolidation, home improvement, medical expenses, and so on.

Rates depend on your creditworthiness, income, and chosen term, and range from approximately
7.5% to 24.9% APR. There is no prepayment penalty — you can pay off your loan early at any time
and only pay interest for the time the loan was outstanding.

Funds are typically disbursed to your account within 1-2 business days of final approval. There
is a one-time origination fee of 1-5% of the loan amount, deducted from the disbursed funds
(this is reflected in your APR, not charged separately).

This is general information, not a rate quote — your actual APR and terms depend on an
individual eligibility assessment.
