# How to open an account

You can open a checking or savings account online, through the mobile app, or at a branch.
Online applications typically take 5-10 minutes. You'll need a government-issued photo ID,
your Social Security number (or equivalent tax ID), a US residential address, and an initial
deposit if the account requires one (most checking accounts have no minimum opening deposit;
savings accounts typically require $25).

Approval is usually instant for online applications. In some cases we may need to verify your
identity manually, which can take 1-2 business days. Once approved, your account number and
routing number are available immediately in the app, and a physical debit card arrives by mail
within 5-7 business days.

You must be 18 or older to open an account individually. Minors can be added as joint owners
on a parent or guardian's account.
