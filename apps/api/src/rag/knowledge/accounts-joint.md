# Joint accounts

A joint account has two or more owners with equal access to view balances, make transactions,
and manage account settings. Any owner can withdraw or transfer the full balance independently
unless the account is specifically set up to require multiple signatures.

To open a joint account, all owners must complete identity verification. You can add a joint
owner to an existing individual account by visiting a branch or submitting a request in the app,
which requires the new owner's consent and ID verification — an existing owner cannot add
someone without their knowledge.

Any owner can remove themselves from a joint account at any time. Removing another owner
generally requires that owner's consent, except in specific legal circumstances (such as a
court order). Both owners' credit profiles are unaffected by standard joint checking or savings
accounts, since these products aren't reported to credit bureaus.
