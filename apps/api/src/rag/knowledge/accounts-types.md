# Checking vs. savings accounts

**Checking accounts** are built for everyday spending: unlimited transactions, a debit card,
check writing, and bill pay. They typically earn little to no interest.

**Savings accounts** are built for setting money aside. They earn interest (rates vary and are
published on our rates page) but are limited to 6 withdrawals or transfers per statement cycle
under federal Regulation D guidance; exceeding this may incur a fee or account conversion.

We also offer a **Money Market account**, which blends features of both: higher interest than
standard savings, limited check-writing ability, and a higher minimum balance requirement
(typically $2,500) to avoid a monthly fee.

You can hold multiple account types under one login and move money between them instantly with
no transfer fee.
