# Loan application process and timeline

1. **Pre-qualification** (optional) — check estimated rates in the app with a soft credit check
   that doesn't affect your credit score. Takes about 1 minute.
2. **Application** — submit income, employment, and existing debt information. Takes 5-10
   minutes.
3. **Credit check and decision** — we run a hard credit inquiry and evaluate your application
   against our eligibility criteria. Most applicants get a decision within minutes; some require
   manual underwriting review, which takes 1-2 business days.
4. **Offer review** — if approved, you'll see your rate, term, and monthly payment before
   accepting. Offers are valid for 14 days.
5. **Funding** — once you accept, funds are disbursed within 1-2 business days.

You'll need: proof of income (pay stubs or tax returns), employment information, and details on
existing debts. Self-employed applicants may need to provide 2 years of tax returns instead of
pay stubs.
