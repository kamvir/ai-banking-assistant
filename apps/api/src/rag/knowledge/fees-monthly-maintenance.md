# Monthly maintenance fee

Our standard checking account has a $10 monthly maintenance fee. This fee is automatically
waived if you meet any one of the following each statement cycle:

- Maintain a minimum daily balance of $1,500, or
- Receive a qualifying direct deposit of $500 or more, or
- Are a student under 24 enrolled in an eligible academic program.

Savings accounts have a $5 monthly fee, waived with a minimum daily balance of $300 or by
linking to an eligible checking account with recurring auto-transfers set up.

The Money Market account has a $12 monthly fee, waived with a minimum daily balance of $2,500.

Fees are assessed on the last business day of the statement cycle and reflected on your next
statement. If a fee causes your balance to go negative, standard overdraft terms apply.
