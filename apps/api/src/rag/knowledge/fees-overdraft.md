# Overdraft fees

If a transaction would take your checking account balance below $0, you have three options,
configurable in the app under Settings > Overdraft Preferences:

1. **Decline the transaction** (default for debit card and ATM transactions unless you opt in
   to overdraft coverage) — no fee is charged, but the transaction won't go through.
2. **Standard overdraft coverage** — the transaction is paid and a $30 overdraft fee applies per
   transaction, up to a maximum of 3 fees ($90) per day.
3. **Linked account transfer** — funds are pulled automatically from a linked savings account to
   cover the shortfall, for a flat $5 transfer fee instead of the $30 overdraft fee.

We provide a 24-hour grace period: if you bring your balance back to $0 or above within one
business day of an overdraft, the fee is automatically refunded, once per rolling 12-month
period. Overdraft fees do not apply to transactions under $5.
