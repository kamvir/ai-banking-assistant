# Updating your KYC information

You're required to keep your identity information current. Update your address, phone number,
or email anytime in the app under Profile > Personal Information — most changes take effect
immediately, no re-verification needed.

Changing your legal name (e.g., after marriage) requires uploading a supporting document (such
as a marriage certificate or court order) along with an updated government ID, since this
triggers a re-verification of your identity.

We may also periodically ask you to reconfirm your information — typically every 1-3 years, or
sooner if your account activity changes significantly (for example, a large increase in
transaction volume) — as part of standard ongoing KYC monitoring required by banking
regulations. You'll get an in-app and email notification if reconfirmation is needed, with a
30-day window to complete it before any account restrictions apply.
