# How loan interest rates are determined

Your interest rate is based on several factors evaluated together, not any single number:

- **Credit score** — generally the biggest factor; higher scores unlock lower rates.
- **Debt-to-income ratio (DTI)** — your total monthly debt payments divided by your gross
  monthly income. Lower DTI signals more capacity to take on new debt.
- **Loan term** — shorter terms often carry slightly lower rates than longer terms.
- **Loan amount and type** — secured loans (like auto loans) typically get lower rates than
  unsecured personal loans, since collateral reduces the lender's risk.
- **Employment and income stability** — steady income history can favorably affect underwriting.

Rates are reviewed and published regularly based on broader market conditions (such as the
prevailing federal funds rate), but your individual offer reflects your personal
creditworthiness at the time you apply, not just the published range. Rates are fixed for the
life of the loan once accepted — they don't change after funding.
